# Privacy Policy **District Google AI Search Hider** **Effective Date:** October 26, 2026 ## Overview District Google AI Search Hider is a Chrome browser extension designed to hide Google AI Overviews and AI Mode entry points while preserving traditional Google Search functionality. ## Information Collection The extension is designed to operate locally within the user's browser. The extension does not collect, store, sell, or transmit personal information, browsing history, search queries, or other user data. ## Website Access The extension operates on Google Search pages to identify and hide selected AI-related interface elements. This access is used solely to provide the extension's functionality. ## Data Sharing The extension does not share user information with third parties. It does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or external data collection services. ## Data Storage The extension does not maintain user accounts or store personal information. ## Children's Privacy The extension is intended to be suitable for use in educational environments, including managed student Chromebooks. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. ## Changes to This Policy This privacy policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data practices change. ## Contact For questions about this privacy policy, contact the extension's publisher using the contact information provided on its Chrome Web Store listing.
